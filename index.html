The problem

The web has records.
Research needs continuity.

A name found on six websites is not the same as an independently proved family relationship. BROPP keeps past investigations useful, separates original documents from syndicated copies and proposes the next source capable of resolving a specific uncertain identity.

I.

Original sources first

Work from official records, archived publications and carefully cited sources rather than confident but unsupported AI assertions.

II.

Identity before pedigree

Preserve namesakes, variant dates and conflicting evidence until an original record supports the connection.

III.

Research that accumulates

Source identifiers, negative searches and case-specific next proofs prevent repeated unproductive browsing.

Research technology

Structured records meet the live web.

The prototype combines a multi-jurisdictional source registry, evidence-linked case data, research queues and a local browser console. TinyFish is being evaluated as a permitted Search, Fetch and bounded browser-access component—not an automatic authority on genealogy.

Illustrative prototype scenario

Open the interactive fictional evidence review ↗

One question, not a thousand guesses.

This fictional example illustrates the workflow without exposing real people's records or identities.

An uncertain historical parentage
  • A newspaper index mentions a family surname and birth year.
  • A separate marriage index supplies a plausible parent, but the maiden name differs.
  • BROPP tracks both readings and the source lineages.
  • The next test is an exact original birth or marriage register.
Review outcome: not yet proved.

The system identifies an archival record that could decide the question. No identity or parent-child relationship is promoted from a secondary hint, and no living person is contacted automatically.

Integrity and privacy

Careful by design.

The research engine is being evaluated on permitted public and historical sources. Original documents, lawful access and human adjudication remain essential. This website contains no private case records, claimant identities, login sessions or account details.

Status: independent prototype, pre-production, not a legal determination service.
